Reflections

Short notes on what I've learned in my first months at Mapúa. Tap an entry to read it.

October 2026

Mapúa tests your time

 +

The biggest thing I've learned so far is that Mapúa really tests your time management. In class the lessons keep coming, and outside class there are requirements, activities, and things I want to read up on myself.

Nobody is going to organize my day for me. When I plan ahead I get through the week fine, and when I don't, everything piles up at once. I'm still working on it, and I expect to keep working on it all year.

October 2026

Failure is part of the process

 +

I play mobile games and sports, and in both you lose a lot before you get good. Nobody wins every match, and I never expected to. I'm bringing that same attitude to my studies.

A bad quiz or a wrong answer doesn't mean I picked the wrong course. It tells me what to review next. That's why my motto is that failure will always be part of the process.

October 2026

Campus resources I'm glad to have

 +

Doing the campus services activity showed me how much support Mapúa already has for students. The library gives me online research materials, so I can read beyond what we cover in class, plus a quiet place to focus before exams.

The gymnasium works differently. Only PathFit classes and student athletes can use it, so I can't walk in whenever I like. Still, I think having a place on campus that keeps students active matters while we're busy with schoolwork.
